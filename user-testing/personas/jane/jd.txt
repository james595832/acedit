Junior Product Designer
Brightpath - London / Hybrid (2 days) - Full-time

About Brightpath
Brightpath builds a learning app for teens preparing for exams. We are a product-led team of 40.

The role
We are hiring a Junior Product Designer to join our core product pod. You will work with a senior designer, a PM, and two engineers on onboarding and weekly practice loops.

What you will do
- Design UI in Figma from briefs and research notes
- Run lightweight usability tests with support from senior designers
- Contribute to our design system components
- Present work in critique and iterate quickly
- Partner with engineers on edge cases and empty states

What we look for
- A portfolio with 1-2 case studies showing process (not only final screens)
- Comfort with Figma and prototyping
- Curiosity about users and willingness to talk to them
- Clear communication and openness to feedback
- Internship or placement experience is a plus; graduation year 2024-2026 welcome

Nice to have
- Interest in education or youth products
- Basic understanding of accessibility
- Experience collaborating with engineers

We offer
- Mentorship from a senior designer
- Competitive junior salary + equity
- Learning budget

Apply with CV and portfolio link.

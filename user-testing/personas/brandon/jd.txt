Head of Design
SummitOps - Remote (UK) / London hub - Full-time

About SummitOps
SummitOps helps mid-market companies run operations workflows. Series C. Design is currently a team of 6 ICs reporting into Product. We are creating a Head of Design role to build the function.

The role
You will lead design across product and brand/product marketing collaboration. You will hire, coach, set standards, and partner with Product and Engineering leadership on strategy. This is a player-coach role at first, shifting toward people leadership as the team grows.

What you will do
- Own the design vision and operating cadence for a growing team
- Hire and develop designers; define leveling and career paths
- Partner with CPO / CTO on roadmap bets and quality tradeoffs
- Raise craft, research maturity, and design system investment
- Represent design in exec forums with clear options and risks
- Occasionally dive deep on highest-stakes product bets

What we look for
- Experience leading designers (direct management preferred)
- Strong product craft background (you can still critique and ship)
- Evidence of building design processes that scale
- Comfort with ambiguity, conflict, and prioritisation under constraint
- Excellent executive communication
- Experience hiring for craft and collaboration

Nice to have
- Experience building a design team from small to mid-size
- B2B / enterprise workflow products
- Experience with research ops and design systems at scale

Compensation
Leadership band + significant equity. We care more about leadership outcomes than title history.

Apply with CV, portfolio, and a short note on how you have grown designers.

Senior Product Designer
Northstar - London / Hybrid - Full-time

About Northstar
Northstar is a B2B workflow platform for operations teams. Series B, ~120 people. Design sits with Product.

The role
We need a Senior Product Designer who can own ambiguous problem spaces end to end, set quality bars, and partner with PMs on roadmap bets. You will not only ship screens; you will frame problems, influence priorities, and coach others.

What you will do
- Lead design for a major product area with high ambiguity
- Run and synthesise research; turn insights into measurable outcomes
- Raise craft and systems thinking across the design team
- Facilitate workshops with product, eng, and GTM
- Present to leadership with clear options and tradeoffs
- Mentor mid / junior designers through critique and pairing

What we look for
- 5+ years product design experience (or equivalent seniority of impact)
- Evidence of owning complex B2B flows with measurable outcomes
- Strong systems thinking and design system contribution
- Comfort influencing roadmap without formal authority
- Excellent storytelling for senior stakeholders
- Experience mentoring designers

Nice to have
- Payments, fintech, or ops tooling experience
- Familiarity with experimentation and analytics
- Whiteboard / onsite facilitation experience

Compensation
Competitive senior band + equity. We hire for impact, not years alone - but we expect senior-level signal in your case studies.

Apply with CV, portfolio, and a short note on a hard tradeoff you owned.
